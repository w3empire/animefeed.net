Infinity Castle Shinobu Joins Demon Slayer: Hinokami Chronicles 2 on April 17

The Insect Hashira arrives as the sixth fighter in the Infinity Castle Part 1 Character Pass. Here is everything included in her new DLC pack.

By Christopher Ivanov
Apr 14, 2026

Sega and Aniplex have confirmed that Shinobu Kocho (Infinity Castle Version) will join the Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba - The Hinokami Chronicles 2 character roster on Friday, April 17, 2026. She arrives as the sixth downloadable fighter in the highly anticipated “Infinity Castle Part 1 Character Pass,” bringing her lethal Insect Breathing techniques to the game’s competitive VS. Mode.

Players can unlock the Insect Hashira either by purchasing the complete character pass or by grabbing her standalone “Shinobu Kocho (Infinity Castle) Character Pack.” Her addition follows a steady stream of film-inspired roster updates, expanding the playable cast with fighters straight out of the recently released Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba Infinity Castle – Part 1: Akaza Returns theatrical film.

Official Announcement (Translated from Japanese): 【Announcement】 The ‘Shinobu Kocho (Infinity Castle)’ Character Pack for Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba - The Hinokami Chronicles 2 officially launches on Friday, April 17! The ‘Infinity Castle - Part 1 Character Pass,’ which includes access to 7 total characters, is available for purchase now.

Now, for fans of the 3D arena fighting genre, this DLC release represents a major shift in the current competitive meta. CyberConnect2 has historically designed alternate character versions with entirely distinct playstyles, meaning this iteration of Shinobu will play very differently from her base-game counterpart.

So, What to Expect from Infinity Castle Shinobu?

The brand-new character trailer highlights exactly why this updated version of the Insect Hashira is a formidable threat.

Standard Shinobu in the first game relied on quick, low-damage thrusts paired with a unique poison mechanic that drained opponent health over time.

The Infinity Castle version takes that foundation and turns up the aggression.

Based on her desperate, high-stakes combat sequences from the recent film, this iteration features much faster dash animations and extended aerial combo routes.

Her movement speed appears optimized to counter heavy-hitting brawlers, allowing players to slip past guard setups and apply constant offensive pressure.

CyberConnect2 is famous for their cinematic ultimate attacks, and the new gameplay footage delivers exactly that.

Shinobu’s new ultimate art features a stunning visual sequence that mirrors the dynamic camera work of the ufotable anime production.

The purple butterfly motifs are brighter, the visual effects fill the screen, and the impact frames give her finishing moves a satisfying sense of weight.

  • Agile Movement: Expect enhanced side-stepping and faster recovery frames after missed attacks.
  • Upgraded Poison Mechanics: Her damage-over-time effects will likely stack differently, punishing opponents who play too defensively.
  • Cinematic Ultimate: A brand-new animation directly adapted from her most intense moments in the Akaza Returns movie.

Tip for Arena Meta: If you played the first game, you know the original Shinobu relied on poking and retreating to let her poison tick down. Unlearn that muscle memory for this version. Based on the new trailer’s frame data, her dash cancels are built to stay right in the enemy’s face. Pair her up with an assist character who restricts movement, like Giyu or Tanjiro, to force your opponent to burn their escape gauges early.

The Complete Infinity Castle Part 1 Character Pass

Publisher Aniplex has been steadily rolling out the fighters included in this specific premium pass since late last year.

The strategy mirrors the episodic release format that fighting games use to keep the player base engaged long after the initial launch window.

If you own the complete pass, Shinobu will automatically unlock on your system on April 17.

Each new fighter introduces fresh matchups and forces veteran players to adapt their defensive strategies.

A Calculated Setup for Doma

Looking at the DLC roadmap, releasing Shinobu right now is a brilliant thematic choice by the developers.

Doma, the Upper Rank Two demon, is the seventh and final character slated for this specific pass, and he is the only one without an official launch date.

Fans of the manga and anime know the deep, tragic history shared between the Insect Hashira and Doma.

Releasing her just before him is the perfect narrative bridge.

When Doma finally drops, players will immediately want to recreate their iconic, devastating clash in the game’s VS. Mode.

We can safely expect Doma’s release window to be announced shortly after Shinobu becomes available, likely targeting a late May or early June launch to cap off the season.

Note on the Doma Matchup: Anyone who tracks the raw digital manga releases knows exactly how the brutal Shinobu and Doma confrontation unfolds. The developers at CyberConnect2 love hiding special “Dramatic Finishes” when canonical rivals face off in VS. Mode. Expect a unique, unannounced cutscene replicating those specific manga panels if you hit Shinobu’s ultimate art directly on Doma. You will definitely want to save your special meter for that setup once he joins the roster.

How Hinokami Chronicles 2 Evolved the Roster

For players who might have missed the launch of the sequel, Hinokami Chronicles 2 significantly expanded upon the foundation laid by the 2021 original.

The base game shipped with over 40 playable characters, carrying over the entire roster from the first title.

This includes all the demons added during the first game’s free post-launch update cycle, as well as the Entertainment District Arc fighters that were originally locked behind paid DLC.

The sequel’s core story mode picks up exactly where the first game left off.

Players get to fight their way through fully rendered 3D environments covering the Entertainment District Arc, the Swordsmith Village Arc, and the Hashira Training Arc.

To sweeten the deal, fans who pre-ordered the game received early unlocks for Muichirō Tokitō and Mitsuri Kanroji.

Plus a free update on September 18 added the ultimate antagonist, Muzan Kibutsuji, to the VS. Mode, giving players the chance to control the progenitor of demons without spending extra money.

Available Editions and Bonus Content

The game officially launched globally on August 5 (with Japan getting it a few days earlier on August 1).

It is currently available across all major current-generation hardware: PlayStation 5, PlayStation 4, Xbox Series X|S, Xbox One, Nintendo Switch, and PC via Steam.

Sega offered several ways to purchase the game, each packed with distinct digital bonuses targeted at dedicated fans:

  • Standard Edition: Includes the base game and the 40+ character roster.
  • Digital Deluxe Edition: This premium version grants two Kimetsu Academy character unlock keys for Kyojuro Rengoku and Tengen Uzui. It also provides four additional character keys for Tengen, Obanai Iguro, Sanemi Shinazugawa, and Gyomei Himejima. Buyers also receive exclusive VS. Mode system voices and alternate in-game costumes.
  • Japanese Special Edition: Dedicated collectors in Japan had access to a physical version that bundled a high-quality figure, exclusive digital costumes, and a special set of physical cases featuring bespoke illustrations drawn directly by the animation studio, ufotable.

Gamers who purchased the English Deluxe Edition were also treated to an early access period, allowing them to jump into the arena up to five days before the standard retail street date.

The Future of Demon Slayer Fighting Games

CyberConnect2 has an established pedigree when it comes to adapting beloved shonen anime into successful 3D arena fighters.

Their work on the Naruto Ultimate Ninja Storm franchise proved that they know how to handle long-running series with expanding casts.

Adapting specific movie versions of characters, like the Infinity Castle variants, allows the studio to update movesets to reflect the characters’ current power levels in the anime without entirely deleting their beginner-friendly base versions.

Shinobu’s arrival is going to inject a lot of speed and technical precision into the current online leaderboards.

Players who enjoy rushdown tactics and punishing slow opponents will definitely want to spend some time in training mode mastering her new combo routes this Friday.

Conclusion (Time to Hit the Training Room)

Honestly, Shinobu’s arrival could not have dropped at a better time for the Hinokami Chronicles 2 community.

The online matchmaking meta was starting to feel a bit predictable, and injecting a high-speed, hyper-aggressive fighter like this new Infinity Castle variant is exactly what we need to scramble the ranked leaderboards.

If you currently main the Insect Hashira, you have some serious homework to do this weekend.

I strongly recommend spending your first hour in the training room just getting comfortable with her updated dash mechanics before queueing up for a live match.

Her fundamental movement is completely reworked from her base-game counterpart.

If you rely on old muscle memory, you are going to drop your combo routes and get punished for it.

Now, that being said, all eyes are officially locked on that final empty character slot.

Giving us Shinobu right now is a wonderfully cruel tease by CyberConnect2, building the hype while we all wait for Doma to officially close out the season pass.

Until the Upper Rank Two demon finally arrives, I will see you in the online lobbies.

Watch out for those poison stacks.

Frequently Asked Questions

When does Infinity Castle Shinobu release in Hinokami Chronicles 2?

The Infinity Castle version of Shinobu Kocho officially launches as DLC on Friday, April 17.

Who is included in the Infinity Castle Part 1 Character Pass?

The premium pass features seven characters directly tied to the recent film: Kaigaku, Doma, and the Infinity Castle versions of Zenitsu, Akaza, Tanjiro, Giyu, and Shinobu.

Can I buy the new Shinobu DLC separately?

Yes. If you do not want to purchase the entire character pass, you can buy her individually through the “Shinobu Kocho (Infinity Castle) Character Pack” on your platform’s digital storefront.

What platforms is Demon Slayer: The Hinokami Chronicles 2 available on?

The game is available to play right now on PlayStation 5, PlayStation 4, Xbox Series X|S, Xbox One, Nintendo Switch, and PC via Steam.

Does the sequel include characters from the first game?

Yes. The base roster of Hinokami Chronicles 2 includes all characters from the first game, including the previously released DLC fighters and the free demon updates.
