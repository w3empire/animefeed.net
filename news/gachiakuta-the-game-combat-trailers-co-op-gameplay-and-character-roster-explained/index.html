Gachiakuta: The Game, Combat Trailers, Co-Op Gameplay, and Character Roster Explained

Beyond the Anime: How Gachiakuta is Delivering a Premium Co-Op Action RPG

By Justin Ali
Apr 2, 2026

Com2uS has officially released a series of new combat trailers for GACHIAKUTA: The Game, showcasing the unique, fast-paced fighting styles of playable characters Rudo, Enjin, Riyo, and Zanka.

Currently in development for PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, and PC via Steam, this upcoming survival action RPG aims to faithfully translate the grim, graffiti-infused world of Kei Urana’s hit manga into a premium, fully 3D multiplayer experience.

If you are wondering whether this adaptation will capture the raw intensity of the 2025 anime, or if it is just another generic arena fighter, the latest reveals point to a robust, mission-based action RPG featuring real-time co-op, base-building mechanics, and intense melee combat.

Here is a comprehensive breakdown of everything we know about GACHIAKUTA: The Game, from character playstyles and newly revealed gameplay loops to system requirements and release details.

Combat Trailer Breakdown: Meet the Cleaners

The latest promotional drops focused heavily on the Cleaners, the specialized combatants who dive into the trash-filled abyss of the Pit to eradicate mutated garbage monsters known as Trash Beasts.

In the Gachiakuta universe, combat revolves around Jinkis, everyday objects imbued with spiritual power by their owners.

The combat trailers reveal how Com2uS has translated these unique weapons into fluid, stylized action.

For a quick overview of the currently revealed roster, check the combat profile summary below:

Character Jinki (Weapon) Primary Combat Style Ideal Player Role
Rudo Gloves Relentless, close-quarters brawling Aggressive rushdown and rapid combo chaining
Enjin Umbrella Sweeping area-of-effect (AoE) Crowd control and mid-range spatial management
Riyo Scissors High-speed, acrobatic mobility Hit-and-run tactics and evasion-based combat
Zanka Staff Methodical, precise heavy strikes Technical spacing and punishing counter-attacks

Rudo: Relentless Brawler

Rudo’s combat trailer highlights a highly aggressive, close-quarters combat style.

Using his gloves as his Jinki, Rudo is designed for players who want to stay in the enemy’s face.

  • Playstyle: Fast, consecutive strikes with high mobility.
  • Best For: Players who prefer rushdown tactics, rapid combo chaining, and overwhelming single targets with sheer speed.

Enjin: Heavy Area-of-Effect (AoE)

Armed with his signature umbrella Jinki, Enjin brings a calculated, heavy-hitting approach to the battlefield.

The trailer showcases his ability to control space, using the umbrella for both sweeping offensive strikes and likely defensive parries.

  • Playstyle: Mid-range spatial control and heavy, sweeping attacks.
  • Best For: Crowd control and managing multiple Trash Beasts at once.

Riyo: Agile and Unpredictable

Wielding her massive scissor Jinki, Riyo’s combat footage demonstrates high-speed, acrobatic mobility.

Her strikes are wide and lethal, designed to slice through enemy ranks before retreating to a safe distance.

  • Playstyle: Hit-and-run tactics with wide-arcing slashes.
  • Best For: Players who enjoy high-risk, high-reward mobility and dodging mechanics.

Zanka: Precise and Measured Strikes

Zanka’s combat trailer specifically emphasizes his “calm yet dangerous posture.”

Armed with his staff, his attacks are methodical, focusing on precise strikes that pack a significant punch, likely dealing heavy stun or stagger damage to larger enemies.

  • Playstyle: Methodical reach, punishing counter-attacks, and tactical spacing.
  • Best For: Technical players who prioritize timing, spacing, and punishing enemy whiff animations.

Gameplay Mechanics: What Kind of Game is Gachiakuta?

So yeah, I know, the biggest concerns among the anime gaming community was the fear of GACHIAKUTA: The Game being a mobile gacha or a simplistic 3D arena brawler.

But official Steam listings and developer updates from AnimeJapan 2026 have confirmed a much deeper gameplay loop.

Survival Action RPG Loop

The game is officially billed as a “Survival Action RPG.”

Players will dive into monster-infested zones within the Pit, fight through hordes of enemies, complete localized missions, and extract with acquired loot.

This implies a gameplay loop closer to a mission-based action game, or even an extraction-lite, where securing resources and returning them safely is just as important as the combat itself.

Real-Time Co-Op Multiplayer

The Steam page officially confirms that GACHIAKUTA: The Game will feature real-time co-op play.

You will not have to cleanse the Pit alone; players can team up with friends to take down massive Trash Beasts, combining different character playstyles (like pairing Rudo’s close-range brawling with Enjin’s AoE control) to maximize efficiency.

Base Building: The Cleaners Headquarters

Adding significant depth to the action is a newly revealed base-building mechanic. Objects and resources acquired during your survival runs in the Pit can be used to customize and develop the Cleaners Headquarters.

  • Upgrades: Expanding the base unlocks new features and establishes a safe stronghold against outlaws.
  • Slice-of-Life: Between intense combat missions, players can engage in slice-of-life interactions with fellow Cleaners, fleshing out the game’s narrative and character relationships.
  • Exploration: The game rewards exploration by scattering hidden graffiti (originally designed by Hideyoshi Ando) throughout the world for players to collect.

The Com2uS Factor

It is impossible to discuss this title without addressing the developer, Com2uS. Historically known for massive mobile gacha hits like Summoners War, their involvement naturally raised eyebrows.

But GACHIAKUTA: The Game is being developed ground-up for current-generation consoles (PS5, Xbox Series X|S) and PC.

The inclusion of premium cartoon-style rendering, robust system requirements, and a survival-loop structure indicates Com2uS is pivoting toward a traditional premium game model for this IP.

While live-service elements (such as seasonal mission updates, cosmetic skins, or new playable characters) are highly likely given the current market, the core design points away from a simple mobile port and toward a dedicated console experience.

The Story: A Quick Catch-Up for Newcomers

If you missed the acclaimed 24-episode anime adaptation that aired in late 2025, or Kei Urana’s original 2022 manga, the premise of Gachiakuta perfectly lends itself to an action RPG.

The story follows Rudo, an orphan living in the slums of a massive floating town.

Society is strictly divided: the wealthy elite live in luxury above, carelessly casting their garbage into the endless abyss below. When Rudo is falsely accused of murder, he faces the ultimate punishment, being thrown over the edge into the Pit.

Instead of dying, he discovers a toxic wasteland where discarded trash has mutated into horrific monsters.

To survive, uncover the truth of his framing, and exact vengeance on the elites above, Rudo joins the Cleaners, a syndicate of outcasts who use their magical Jinkis to fight back the monster hordes.

It is a story about finding value in what is thrown away, heavily stylized with street-culture and graffiti aesthetics.

Platforms, Specs, and Release Window

GACHIAKUTA: The Game is currently listed as “Coming Soon,” with no official release date cemented by Com2uS.

But given the polished state of the combat trailers shown during AnimeJapan 2026, a late 2026 or early 2027 release window is highly probable.

The game will launch on PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, and PC (Steam).

For PC players, Com2uS has already provided the official system requirements, which show the game is reasonably optimized but will require modern hardware to push its high-fidelity cel-shaded graphics at higher framerates.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What type of game is GACHIAKUTA: The Game?

It is a 3D survival action RPG that blends fast-paced, real-time melee combat with mission-based exploration and base-building mechanics.

Is Gachiakuta: The Game multiplayer?

Yes. The official Steam page confirms the game supports real-time co-op play, allowing you to team up with friends to explore the Pit and fight Trash Beasts together.

Is the Gachiakuta game a gacha?

While developer Com2uS is famous for gacha games, GACHIAKUTA: The Game is being developed for PS5, Xbox Series, and PC as a premium 3D action RPG. There has been no official confirmation of gacha mechanics for unlocking characters, though cosmetic microtransactions are common in modern RPGs.

Will the game follow the story of the anime?

Yes, the developers have stated the game aims to fully recreate the world of the anime in full 3D, allowing players to experience the atmosphere, interact with characters, and fight using the Jinki power system seen in the manga and show.

When is Gachiakuta: The Game coming out?

Com2uS has not yet announced a definitive release date. It is currently listed as “Coming Soon” for PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, and PC.
