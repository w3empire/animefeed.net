IShowSpeed's Anime Debut: How Netflix's 'One Piece' Writer is Adapting the YouTube Star

Darren Watkins Jr. is trading live streams for scripted animation. With veteran Hollywood producers and an indie auteur attached, the 21-year-old creator is building his own franchise at Big Shot Pictures.

By Justin Ali
Apr 9, 2026

YouTube star Darren Jason Watkins Jr., widely known to audiences as IShowSpeed, is officially entering the animation industry.

The internet celebrity will lend his voice and physical likeness to a new narrative-driven anime series produced by Big Shot Pictures. Former Netflix One Piece live-action co-showrunner Matt Owens is writing the script, establishing a highly credentialed creative team for the streamer’s television debut.

The 21-year-old creator, who claimed consecutive Streamer of the Year trophies in 2024 and 2025, confirmed his starring role in a recent press statement. This project represents a pivot for the online personality.

He is transitioning from spontaneous IRL (in-real-life) broadcasts to a structured, episodic format alongside veteran Hollywood producer Brian Robbins and acclaimed indie director Harmony Korine.

The Core Concept (Translating Internet Chaos to Scripted Media)

Adapting an unscripted digital persona into a cohesive storyline presents unique challenges.

Audiences tune into a live stream specifically for unpredictability.

Scripted television requires pacing, character arcs, and thematic resolution.

The studio has kept official plot details strictly under wraps.

But sources indicate the production will feature an animated protagonist directly inspired by the streamer himself.

For the creator, this represents a major career milestone.

He noted that working in this medium has been a lifelong aspiration, stating the creative team is exceptionally talented.

His global fanbase of over 52 million YouTube subscribers provides an instant, built-in audience for the upcoming package.

The executives driving this project bring decades of high-level film and television experience.

Matt Owens’ Animation Comeback

Fans of Japanese media recognize Matt Owens for his crucial role in adapting the One Piece manga for western audiences.

He guided the wildly successful first two seasons of the Netflix adaptation before stepping down late last year to prioritize his mental health.

Now the writer takes on a completely different challenge.

Crafting a script around an unpredictable internet celebrity requires a distinct skill set compared to adapting beloved, decades-old source material.

Owens brings much-needed structural discipline to a project that could easily devolve into a string of disconnected internet jokes.

He will also serve as a producer on the series.

Brian Robbins and the Vision for Big Shot Pictures

Brian Robbins exited his role as co-CEO of Paramount Global following the Skydance merger in August 2025.

Unwilling to sit on the sidelines, he launched Big Shot Pictures early this year.

During his Paramount tenure, he supervised blockbuster family franchises like Sonic the Hedgehog, SpongeBob SquarePants, and Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles.

Robbins is now focusing heavily on creator-led intellectual property.

His new venture secured a lucrative first-look distribution deal with Sony Pictures Entertainment, covering both animated and hybrid films.

Backed by investments from firms like Greycroft and ValueAct Capital, the executive is betting that digital icons represent the future of entertainment franchises.

The Harmony Korine Factor

Including filmmaker Harmony Korine (Spring Breakers, Gummo) as a producer introduces a wildcard element.

Korine recently praised the 21-year-old streamer, calling him an “auteur of the future” and comparing his cultural impact to legendary Russian director Andrei Tarkovsky.

Korine’s involvement hints that the art direction will not settle for standard Saturday morning cartoon tropes.

Viewers should anticipate highly kinetic visuals, surreal humor, and an avant-garde approach to the animation itself.

Predicting the Plot (What Will the Anime Look Like?)

While the exact genre remains unconfirmed, industry trends and the creator’s known interests offer strong clues about the direction of this series.

Big Shot Pictures is preparing to shop the package to distributors soon.

  • Sports Anime Mechanics: The influencer’s intense obsession with global soccer, specifically Cristiano Ronaldo, will almost certainly influence the lore. Expect high-stakes athletic showdowns featuring dramatic, over-the-top animation sequences.
  • Hyper-Kinetic Pacing: A show built around a famously loud personality demands fast-paced storytelling. The animation style will likely rely on extreme visual exaggeration and meme-worthy facial expressions.
  • Gaming Crossovers: Video game culture is foundational to the star’s rise. The narrative could easily incorporate isekai elements, dropping the protagonist into virtual worlds.
  • Cross-Cultural Appeal: The series must transcend language barriers. The creator’s fanbase spans multiple continents, meaning the visual storytelling has to translate easily without relying entirely on complex English dialogue.

The Financial Strategy (Why Studios Are Banking on Influencers)

We are observing a fundamental shift in how production companies greenlight new properties.

Relying entirely on established comic books or legacy toy lines is no longer the only viable path to profitability.

Executives now scrutinize social media metrics to calculate financial risk.

A personality boasting 52 million loyal followers presents a much safer investment than an untested original concept.

Traditional marketing campaigns cost tens of millions of dollars to generate basic public awareness.

IShowSpeed can achieve that exact reach with a single post.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is the new IShowSpeed anime about?

While specific plot details are currently classified, the series will feature a narrative-driven story centering on an animated version of the popular YouTube streamer. It will highlight his energetic internet persona.

Who is writing the IShowSpeed animated series?

Matt Owens is writing the script. He is best known for his previous work as the co-showrunner on the first two seasons of the Netflix live-action adaptation of One Piece.

What studio is producing the show?

Big Shot Pictures is the primary production company. Former Paramount co-CEO Brian Robbins founded the studio in early 2026. The company currently holds a distribution deal with Sony Pictures Entertainment.

Will Darren Watkins Jr. voice his own character?

Yes. The 21-year-old creator has confirmed he will serve as the lead voice actor, bringing his authentic voice and personality to his animated counterpart.

When does the IShowSpeed anime release?

There is no official release date yet. The production team is actively developing the series and preparing to pitch the finalized package to major streaming platforms and distributors.
