Everything We Know About The Level 999 Villager Anime: July 2026 Premiere and Seiyuu Roster

Studio Brain's Base brings the hit fantasy light novel to TV Tokyo this summer. Get the full class-system breakdown and voice cast lineup, including new addition Yuichiro Umehara.

By Christopher Ivanov
Apr 9, 2026

The upcoming Summer 2026 fantasy anime The Level 999 Villager has officially cast veteran voice actor Yuichiro Umehara to play Menou, a high-ranking demon noble.

Slated for a July 2026 premiere on TV Tokyo and BS Fuji, the series adapts the popular light novel about a structurally powerless civilian who shatters his world’s level cap to forge a controversial alliance with the Demon King’s daughter.

Fans anticipating the summer broadcasting season can expect a fresh spin on the overpowered protagonist trope. Animation studio Brain’s Base is spearheading the adaptation, promising a mix of high-stakes action and sharp comedic timing.

Below is a complete breakdown of the new cast additions, the unique mechanics of the show’s fantasy world, and upcoming promotional events you need to mark on your calendar.

Why Yuichiro Umehara is the Perfect Fit for Menou

The addition of Yuichiro Umehara, widely recognized for playing cool, calculating characters like Saiga Harima in Kusunoki’s Garden of Gods, brings immediate credibility to the demon faction.

Menou is not a disposable villain.

He is a pragmatic, highly intelligent aristocrat whose family has served the Demon King for generations.

Umehara’s performance promises a highly entertaining dynamic.

According to the actor’s official commentary, Menou acts as the frustrated straight man to the protagonist’s chaotic energy.

  • The Comedic Foil: Menou consistently finds himself overwhelmed and pushed around by Kagami’s rule-breaking abilities.
  • Character Growth: Through these comedic interactions, Menou gradually shifts his worldview, becoming a key advocate for human-demon coexistence.
  • Vocal Range: Expect Umehara to pivot sharply between deadpan humor and intense, dramatic monologues.

The Class System of Hexaldoria Explained

Most fantasy anime rely on soft magic systems, but The Level 999 Villager operates on a rigid, birth-assigned caste system.

The island nation of Hexaldoria (formerly Japan) is overrun by monsters, making 80% of the world uninhabitable.

Survival dictates that every citizen adheres to their assigned role.

The hierarchy is split into three distinct tiers:

Table: The Hexaldoria Class System and Societal Hierarchy

Class Tier Societal Role Specific Professions Inherent Combat Ability
Oracle (Special) Leaders and world-saviors. King, Sage, Hero Maximum: Capable of altering the world’s balance.
Competent (Standard) Backbone of the military and economy. Fighter, Hunter, Cleric, Warrior, Mage, Merchant, Thief High: Specialized skills tailored for monster subjugation and survival.
Villager (Weakest) Civilians requiring constant protection. Villager Zero: Physically incapable of fighting. (Exception: Kouji Kagami)

The primary hook of the narrative hinges on Kouji Kagami.

Born as a lowly Villager, Kagami somehow bypassed the world’s limitations to achieve Level 999.

His existence threatens the established political order, especially once he aligns himself with Alice, the daughter of the Demon Lord.

Behind the Scenes: Animation Studio and Core Staff

A strong voice cast requires equally competent animation staff.

Brain’s Base, the studio famous for culturally impactful hits like Baccano! and Natsume’s Book of Friends, is handling the production.

Their involvement suggests a strong focus on character acting and atmospheric world-building.

The core production team includes industry veterans:

  • Director: Yoshinobu Kasai
  • Series Composition: Shinzo Fujita
  • Character Design: Kentaro Matsumoto
  • Music: Hiroshi Nakamura

This specific combination of talent indicates the show will balance the lighter, slice-of-life comedy elements with the darker realities of a world constantly at war.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

When does The Level 999 Villager anime premiere?

The series will begin broadcasting in July 2026. Episodes will air on TV Tokyo and BS Fuji in Japan, with western streaming platform simulcasts (like Crunchyroll or HIDIVE) expected to be announced shortly.

Who wrote the original light novel?

The series was written by Koneko Hoshitsuki with original character illustrations by Fuumi. It originally launched as a web novel in 2015 before being picked up for print publication by Enterbrain.

How many volumes of the light novel are there?

The Japanese print run concluded with exactly nine volumes. There is also an ongoing manga adaptation illustrated by Kenichi Iwamoto, which is currently available in English through MangaPlaza.

Why is Kagami considered a threat if he is just a Villager?

In the world of Earth Clear, Villagers are physically incapable of fighting monsters. Kagami broke the natural laws of his reality by grinding his stats up to Level 999, making him functionally immortal and stronger than the highest-ranking Heroes.
