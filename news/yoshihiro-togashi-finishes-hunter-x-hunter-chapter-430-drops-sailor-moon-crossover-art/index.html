Yoshihiro Togashi Completes Hunter x Hunter Chapter 430 Inks

The manga legend has enough material ready to fill three graphic novels, and he is celebrating the milestone with a rare crossover sketch thanking his wife.

By Christopher Ivanov
Apr 8, 2026

Yoshihiro Togashi has officially finished inking Hunter x Hunter Chapter 430. Fans waiting for the beloved series to resume can expect new issues soon, as the creator confirmed that chapters 420 and beyond are currently awaiting a specific publication date from Weekly Shonen Jump.

He shared this major production update alongside a rare crossover illustration featuring characters from Sailor Moon. The sketch serves as a public thank you to his wife, Sailor Moon creator Naoko Takeuchi, for giving him the physical and emotional strength to keep drawing during his ongoing health struggles.

This new art breaks from his usual social media habit of posting blank, dry-erase whiteboard corners.

The finished inks prove a substantial block of manga is ready for the printing press.

The Rare Hunter x Hunter and Sailor Moon Crossover

Togashi surprised his social media followers by posting a sketch that bridges two of the most profitable franchises in Japanese history.

The illustration features Kurapika standing alongside Sailor Mars and Artemis.

The artwork contains several hidden details for sharp-eyed readers:

Table: Visual and Textual Easter Eggs in Togashi’s Crossover Post

Illustration Element Franchise / Origin Deeper Significance
Artemis as Maneki-Neko Sailor Moon The “lucky cat” holds a coin reading “Thanks,” a direct message of gratitude to Naoko Takeuchi.
“4/17” Charm Sailor Moon Lore Represents April 17th, the official canon birthday of Rei Hino (Sailor Mars).
#Artemis2 Hashtag Real-World Science Points to NASA’s upcoming lunar flyby, aligning with Takeuchi’s heavy use of astronomy motifs.
#ShiningTheater Hashtag Real-World Business Direct promotion for Takeuchi’s current live-action Sailor Moon dinner theater project in Japan.

Togashi captioned the image by stating he receives continuous energy from his wife.

He also included two distinct hashtags: #Artemis2 and #ShiningTheater.

Decoding the Hashtags

These hashtags point directly to Takeuchi’s current interests and business ventures. #Artemis2 refers to the upcoming NASA lunar flyby mission.

Space and astronomy remain central themes in the Sailor Moon universe.

#ShiningTheater promotes a highly specific, live-action Japanese dinner show based on his wife’s work.

Highlighting her projects on his heavily followed X account (formerly Twitter) shows a mutual exchange of promotional support.

What Chapter 430 Means for the Hiatus

The Hunter x Hunter manga remains officially on hiatus. The last published installment, Chapter 400, hit newsstands in December 2022.

Reaching the end of Chapter 430 indicates Togashi has stockpiled enough material for a long, uninterrupted run.

Table: Hunter x Hunter Manga Production Pipeline & Current Status

Chapter Range Current Production Status Release Format
Chapters 391–400 Published (December 2022) Compiled in Volume 38
Chapters 401–410 Inking & Backgrounds Completed Next expected release batch
Chapters 411–420 Inking Completed Awaiting editorial schedule
Chapters 421–430 Inks Completed (Latest Update) Pending assistant finalization

Chronic back issues have forced the author into extended breaks for over a decade.

He physically cannot sit in a traditional desk chair. Togashi frequently draws while lying completely flat on his back.

Inking a page is the most physically demanding part of creating a comic book.

Completing the inks for 10 full chapters guarantees that his assistants can now finalize the backgrounds and screentones without waiting on him.

Shueisha’s New Publication Strategy

Readers should not expect a standard weekly release.

Weekly Shonen Jump editors announced in 2022 that Hunter x Hunter would no longer follow a weekly serialization format due to the author’s medical condition.

The magazine created a customized release schedule for the series.

Togashi explicitly told fans to “keep an eye out for an announcement by the Jump editors” regarding the exact start date.

The editorial department typically waits until 10 or 20 chapters are completely finished before scheduling a return.

This strategy prevents mid-arc delays and ensures readers get a complete volume’s worth of story at one time.

The Power Couple Behind the Manga

Yoshihiro Togashi and Naoko Takeuchi married in 1999.

They operate as the quiet royalty of the Japanese comic industry. Despite their combined global success, they rarely collaborate publicly.

Takeuchi occasionally steps in to help as an uncredited assistant.

During the grueling deadlines of the Greed Island arc in the early 2000s, she actively helped apply screentones to her husband’s pages.

Togashi directly crediting her support on social media provides a rare look into their private dynamic.

Fans rarely get to see the two creators acknowledge their shared artistic life, making this recent sketch a notable piece of pop culture history.

Tracking the Current Story Arc

When the series returns with Chapter 401, it will pick up in the middle of the Succession Contest arc.

This storyline takes place on the Black Whale, a massive ship headed toward the Dark Continent.

The plot involves a complex battle royale between the Kakin Empire princes, the Phantom Troupe, and the Zodiacs.

Kurapika is the central protagonist of this current arc, which explains his prominent placement in the recent crossover sketch.

Togashi usually writes incredibly dense, text-heavy chapters for this specific storyline.

The fact that he managed to finish the line art for an entire volume of complex political thriller content while battling severe sciatica is a notable achievement.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

When is Hunter x Hunter Chapter 401 releasing?

Weekly Shonen Jump has not announced a specific release date yet. Yoshihiro Togashi confirmed he finished working on material up to Chapter 430, meaning the magazine editors will likely announce the official return date soon.

Did Sailor Moon’s creator draw the new Hunter x Hunter art?

No. Yoshihiro Togashi drew the crossover illustration himself. He included Sailor Mars and Artemis as a tribute to his wife, Naoko Takeuchi, thanking her for helping him push through his workload.

Why does Hunter x Hunter go on hiatus so often?

The creator suffers from debilitating back pain. His condition often prevents him from sitting at a desk, forcing him to draw lying down. Shueisha moved the manga off its strict weekly schedule to accommodate his health.

What do the Artemis2 and ShiningTheater hashtags mean?

Togashi used his platform to promote his wife’s interests. #Artemis2 references the real-world NASA moon mission, fitting the space theme of her work. #ShiningTheater promotes a current live-action Sailor Moon dinner show in Japan.

How many chapters are usually in a Hunter x Hunter volume?

A standard Japanese manga volume contains exactly 10 chapters. Because the last hiatus ended at Chapter 400, finishing the inks for Chapter 430 means Togashi has effectively prepared three full graphic novels worth of new content.
