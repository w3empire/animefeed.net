Another Eden Begins Arrives Sept. 17: Switch 2 Details and New Game+ Explained

The hit mobile RPG sheds its gacha mechanics for a premium, fully voiced console remake arriving this September.

By Justin Ali
Apr 5, 2026

Another Eden Begins will officially launch on September 17, 2026, for the Nintendo Switch, Nintendo Switch 2, and PC (Steam). Rather than a simple port, this is a premium, ground-up console remake of the first story arc from the hit mobile RPG Another Eden: The Cat Beyond Time and Space. Featuring a completely redesigned combat system, full voice acting, and a multi-ending “New Game+” mode, it promises to be a standalone experience free from mobile gaming mechanics.

The announcement, delivered during the Another Eden Spring Festival 2026 livestream, confirms that developer Wright Flyer Studios is taking the franchise in a bold new direction.

Longtime mobile fans and RPG enthusiasts alike are eyeing the same thing: a rich, time-traveling narrative. Here is everything you need to know about the upcoming release.

A Complete Overhaul, Not Just a Port

When mobile games make the jump to consoles, they are often direct ports. Another Eden Begins breaks that mold entirely.

Feature Another Eden (Mobile/Original) Another Eden Begins (Console Remake)
Monetization Free-to-Play with Gacha mechanics Full-price Premium Release + Paid DLC
Story Scope Endless, ongoing live-service updates Self-contained “Part 1” narrative with a definitive end
Voice Acting Partial (combat barks and key scenes) 100% Fully Voiced Main Scenario
Combat Mechanics Traditional Turn-Based & Valor Chants Redesigned with Chain Skills & Variable Change
Endgame Focus Material grinding and daily logins Replayability via 10+ “What If” New Game+ Endings
Character Roster Hundreds of gacha characters 19 Base Characters (+10 via DLC Pack)

The developers have stated that field exploration, combat, and character growth systems have been rebuilt from scratch.

The original Another Eden launched on mobile devices nine years ago. While it was praised for its traditional, single-player JRPG approach, it was still built on a mobile framework.

Begins strips away those limitations.

Key upgrades include:

  • Full Voice Acting: For the first time, the entire main story script will be fully voiced, bringing new emotional weight to the narrative.
  • New Scenario Interpretations: Legendary writer Masato Kato (best known for his work on Chrono Trigger and Final Fantasy VII) has returned to the script. He is adding “new interpretations” to the original Part 1 storyline, fleshing out character arcs and expanding lore.
  • Console-Optimized Progression: The mobile game’s progression was tied to gacha mechanics and long grinds. Begins redesigns character growth to match the pacing of a traditional $60 premium console RPG.

Combat and Gameplay Upgrades

The newly released launch trailer teased major shifts in how battles play out.

The mobile game relied heavily on standard turn-based combat and a reserve-swapping mechanic called “Valor Chants.”

Another Eden Begins introduces two new core combat pillars: Chain Skills and Variable Change.

  • Chain Skills likely point toward combo attacks between specific party members, rewarding players who build synergistic teams.
  • Variable Change appears to be an evolution of the reserve-swapping mechanic, allowing characters to apply massive buffs or debuffs the moment they tag into the frontline.

These additions suggest a faster, more dynamic combat flow designed specifically for a gamepad rather than a touchscreen.

The Multi-Ending “New Game+” System

Perhaps the most exciting announcement is the addition of a robust New Game+ mode unlocked after completing the main campaign.

This post-game content features over 10 different “What If” scenarios, leading to multiple distinct endings.

Given that Another Eden is a game entirely centered around time travel, and writer Masato Kato pioneered the “New Game+ multi-ending” concept back in 1995 with Chrono Trigger, this is a massive selling point.

Players will be able to use their endgame characters to explore alternate timelines and see how different choices ripple across history.

Pre-Order Details, Editions, and DLC

Wright Flyer Studios is offering several ways to purchase the game, ranging from a standard digital release to high-end physical collector’s boxes.

Physical and Collector’s Editions

Physical editions of the game are available for both the original Nintendo Switch and the upcoming Nintendo Switch 2.

  • Standard Edition: The base physical game. Pre-orders open at major retailers (like Amazon and Best Buy) on April 9, 2026.
  • Collection Box: A mid-tier premium edition packed with extra physical goodies, also available starting April 9, 2026.
  • Special Collection Box: A highly limited, top-tier collector’s edition. This version is only available via the Wright Flyer Store (in Japanese), and pre-orders are live right now.

Tip: All physical and digital editions of Another Eden Begins include an “Early Purchase Bonus” in the form of a serial code. This code can be redeemed in the original mobile game, Another Eden: The Cat Beyond Time and Space, for exclusive in-game rewards.

DLC Details: Additional Character Pack

Because the mobile version of Another Eden features hundreds of unlockable characters, the development team had to make choices about who makes the cut for the console remake.

The base game of Another Eden Begins will feature 19 playable companions carefully woven into the main narrative.

But the studio also announced a post-launch “Additional Character Pack” DLC. This premium add-on will introduce 10 extra characters to the game.

While Wright Flyer Studios has not yet revealed which fan-favorite characters are included in the DLC, they advise players to wishlist the game on Steam and follow official social channels for upcoming roster reveals.

So, Another Eden Begins?

From an industry perspective, Another Eden Begins represents a fascinating shift.

We rarely see a highly successful mobile “gacha” game get reverse-engineered into a traditional, premium console release.

Preserving Game History

Mobile games are inherently ephemeral. When servers eventually shut down, the games disappear forever.

Wright Flyer Studios is preserving Masato Kato’s legacy.

Rebuilding the first arc of Another Eden as an offline title ensures the story survives long after the servers eventually go dark.

The Nintendo Switch 2 Factor

Launching on September 17, 2026, this title is positioning itself as an early RPG option for the Nintendo Switch 2.

The developers are playing both sides of the hardware cycle. Launching on the current Switch secures a huge audience, while the new hardware version provides the resolution and framerate upgrades fans expect from next-gen tech.

A Focused Narrative

In a live-service mobile game, the story must constantly expand to keep players engaged over years.

Limiting the roster to 19 characters and focusing strictly on ‘Part 1’ makes Another Eden Begins feel like a focused, intentional RPG rather than a sprawling mobile port.

Players can expect a tightly paced adventure with a clear beginning, middle, and end, free from the padding usually required by mobile game event schedules.

Mobile Game News: The 9th Anniversary Campaign

While the console game stole the show, the original mobile game, Another Eden: The Cat Beyond Time and Space, is celebrating a massive milestone. During the Spring 2026 broadcast, the team outlined major updates for the 9th Anniversary.

9,000 Chronos Stones Giveaway

To celebrate nine years of operation, the developers are launching a highly generous campaign.

Players who log in during the anniversary window can earn up to 9,000 Chronos Stones (the game’s premium currency).

This is an excellent opportunity for lapsed players to return and pull for the newest characters.

New Major Content: Apocrypha

The livestream also revealed a brand-new large-scale serialized story event.

Titled “Apocrypha: The Wandering Soul and the Voyager of the Reverse Star”, this new chapter promises to delve deeper into the game’s complex multiverse.

Longtime players can expect new dungeons, new bosses, and significant lore drops tying into the broader Another Eden universe.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

When does Another Eden Begins come out?

The game will be released globally on September 17, 2026.

What platforms is Another Eden Begins available on?

The game is confirmed for the Nintendo Switch, Nintendo Switch 2, and PC via Steam. Currently, there is no official word on PlayStation or Xbox releases.

Do I need to play the mobile game to understand the story?

No. Another Eden Begins is a complete ground-up remake of the first arc of the mobile game. It is designed to be the perfect entry point for brand-new players, featuring a self-contained story and a completely overhauled combat system.

Is there a gacha system in Another Eden Begins?

No. Unlike the mobile version, Begins is a premium console game. You unlock the core 19 characters through natural gameplay progression. There is an optional “Additional Character Pack” DLC that adds 10 more characters, but it is a direct purchase, not a randomized gacha pull.

What is the connection to Chrono Trigger?

Another Eden was originally created and written by Masato Kato, who was a key writer and director for the legendary 1995 RPG Chrono Trigger. Both games share heavy themes of time travel, jumping between eras (like antiquity, the present, and the future) to stop world-ending threats, and feature multiple “what if” endings.
