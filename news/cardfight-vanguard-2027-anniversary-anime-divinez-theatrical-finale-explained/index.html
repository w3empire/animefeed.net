Cardfight!! Vanguard Announces 2027 Anniversary Anime and Divinez Theatrical Finale

Bushiroad outlined the future of its flagship card game, announcing a cinematic finale for the current 'D Series' in October 2026, followed by a brand-new television project.

By Justin Ali
Apr 11, 2026

Bushiroad confirmed a brand-new television series for the Cardfight!! Vanguard franchise will premiere in 2027 to celebrate the property’s 15th anniversary.

Ahead of this major milestone, the current Divinez storyline will conclude with an advance theatrical film, titled Cardfight!! Vanguard Divinez Unmei Seisen-hen (The Fated Star Battle Arc), opening in Japanese cinemas on October 2, 2026.

The trading card game giant made these announcements immediately following the broadcast of the Genma Seisen-hen (Phantom Star Battle Arc) finale on April 11, 2026. This strategic pivot moves the long-running “D Series” narrative from standard television broadcasts to the big screen for its climactic chapter.

The upcoming movie serves as the sixth and final chapter of the Divinez saga.

Shifting a television finale to a cinematic release represents a calculated upgrade in production value.

Theatrical anime screenings typically feature higher animation budgets, uncompromised pacing, and exclusive promotional merchandise distributed directly to ticket holders.

Kinema Citrus and CLAMP Return

The studio Kinema Citrus retains control over the animation production for The Fated Star Battle Arc.

This animation house previously built a strong reputation working on hits like Made in Abyss and The Rising of the Shield Hero.

Their continued involvement guarantees visual consistency for longtime viewers.

Renowned manga collective CLAMP will once again provide the original character designs.

Famous for iconic properties like Cardcaptor Sakura and Code Geass, CLAMP’s distinct art style gave the entire “D Series” a sleek, modernized aesthetic that helped attract a broader, general audience beyond hardcore card game players.

American fans should anticipate a delay of three to six months before Unmei Seisen-hen becomes legally available online with English subtitles or a dubbed version.

A Decade and a Half of TCG Dominance (The 2027 Project)

Very few anime properties sustain continuous broadcasts for fifteen years. The 2027 television series represents a fresh starting point for the franchise.

Bushiroad carefully designed this future project to commemorate the original 2011 debut while preparing the brand for the next generation of players.

Realistic Speculation on the 15th Anniversary

Trading card game producers routinely use anniversary milestones to execute soft reboots.

Introducing a completely original protagonist and establishing fresh game mechanics lowers the barrier to entry for beginners.

Expect the 2027 anime to abandon the current Divinez continuity entirely.

A clean slate allows Bushiroad to market physical starter decks to young American consumers without requiring them to watch ten previous seasons of backstory.

The company will likely reveal the new main character and card formats during their annual strategic presentation in early 2027.

The Complete “D Series” Timeline Explained

Bushiroad initially promised fans exactly nine seasons for the “D Series” narrative block.

High viewership and strong physical card sales prompted executives to extend the roadmap well past that original boundary.

The Divinez era alone expanded into multiple complex tournament arcs. Navigating the watch order requires a clear map.

The Early Years (overDress and will+Dress)

The current continuity began in the spring of 2021.

This era established the core cast and introduced the Dress mechanic to the tabletop game.

Where to Stream Cardfight!! Vanguard Right Now

Crunchyroll holds the primary streaming rights for the franchise in North America.

The platform currently hosts the first two seasons of Divinez for premium subscribers.

Due to complex licensing agreements, regional restrictions apply.

The service actively blocks access to these specific episodes in Japan, China, and Korea, where local television networks maintain exclusive broadcast rights.

American viewers looking to catch up before the 2027 anniversary reboot can access the earlier overDress and will+Dress seasons through Crunchyroll’s catalog.

The platform offers both original Japanese audio with English subtitles and dedicated English dubs for select story arcs.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

When does the new Cardfight!! Vanguard anime come out?

Bushiroad scheduled the brand-new 15th-anniversary television series to premiere in 2027. The exact month remains unannounced. Prior to that, the Divinez theatrical finale opens in Japan on October 2, 2026.

Is Cardfight!! Vanguard Divinez finished?

The television broadcast portion ended on April 11, 2026, with the conclusion of the Genma Seisen-hen arc. The overarching story will officially conclude in the upcoming October 2026 movie, Unmei Seisen-hen.

Do I need to watch overDress before Divinez?

You can start watching Divinez without prior knowledge, but understanding the character relationships requires watching overDress and will+Dress first. The upcoming 2027 series will likely be a completely fresh starting point requiring zero prior viewing.

Where can I watch the Cardfight Vanguard Unmei Seisen-hen movie?

The movie will exclusively screen in Japanese theaters starting October 2, 2026. International streaming availability on platforms like Crunchyroll typically follows several months after the domestic theatrical run concludes.

What studio animates Cardfight!! Vanguard?

Kinema Citrus currently handles the animation production for the modern “D Series” block, utilizing character designs drafted by the famous manga artist group CLAMP.
