Shuten Order’s Physical Switch and Switch 2 Release Delayed in North America

Supply chain bottlenecks push the North American retail launch back one week, while European shipments remain on schedule for April 23.

By Justin Ali
Apr 8, 2026

North American fans waiting to secure a boxed copy of Shuten Order will need to hold out one extra week. PM Studios and publisher Spike Chunsoft have officially pushed the physical release date for both the Nintendo Switch and Nintendo Switch 2 editions to April 30, 2026, abandoning the original April 23 target.

This delay specifically impacts retail stock in the United States and Canada due to late-stage supply chain complications. European players can breathe easy, as the region’s physical Switch 2 copies will still hit store shelves as originally planned on April 23.

If you are wondering how this impacts the overall availability of the game, the digital versions remain completely unaffected. You can already buy the title on the eShop and Steam, as it launched globally last fall.

The Updated Release Schedule at a Glance

Retail logistics vary wildly across regions, especially during a console generation overlap.

Here is the revised physical launch timeline for the Too Kyo Games mystery:

  • North America (Switch & Switch 2): April 30, 2026
  • Europe (Switch 2 only): April 23, 2026

Notice the hardware split.

In Europe, distributor REEF Entertainment is exclusively handling the Switch 2 retail run.

North American players, supplied by PM Studios, have the option to buy either the standard Switch cartridge or the newer Switch 2 version.

What Caused the Shipping Hiccup?

The official press release cites “ongoing global shipping disruptions outside of our control.”

While corporate speak often obscures the real issue, physical media collectors understand this reality well.

Printing two different cartridge formats for a niche Japanese import creates complex freight hurdles.

Smaller print runs routinely get bumped down the priority list at international shipping ports.

Moving freight across the Pacific Ocean involves customs clearances, port congestion, and limited cargo space.

A one-week delay is a relatively minor setback compared to the months-long inventory holds the gaming industry suffered a few years ago.

Switch vs. Switch 2 Editions (Pricing and Differences)

Consumers looking to buy the box will notice a clear price divide between the two Nintendo platforms.

The standard Nintendo Switch version retails for $49.99 in North America.

Upgrading to the Nintendo Switch 2 physical edition bumps the cost to $59.99 (matching the €59.99 and £54.99 European price tags).

Spike Chunsoft has not detailed every granular technical difference between the two cartridges.

But the ten-dollar premium on the new hardware generally accounts for faster load times, higher resolution asset packages, and smoother frame pacing during complex visual sequences.

If you plan to play the game on older hardware, the $49.99 option offers a perfectly viable entry point.

Early adopters of the Switch 2 will naturally gravitate toward the $59.99 version to utilize their new system’s processing overhead.

What is Shuten Order?

For those who missed the digital launch in September 2025, Shuten Order (also known as Shuten Kyodan in Japan) is a dark, text-heavy thriller.

The plot kicks off in a fanatical religious nation.

Followers discover their revered Founder murdered and cleanly dismembered. This gruesome inciting incident spirals into a deep conspiracy.

The gameplay structure breaks the narrative into five distinct adventure game segments, layering different mechanics over a central mystery.

You spend your time reading testimonies, searching for clues, and untangling a web of ideological motives.

It requires patience, careful reading, and a stomach for macabre storytelling.

The Creative Masterminds Behind the Mystery

The main reason this game commands a dedicated physical release is the talent attached to it.

Too Kyo Games assembled a veteran crew of visual novel creators to build this world.

Kazutaka Kodaka serves as the marquee name.

He created the Danganronpa franchise, a series that redefined the modern murder mystery genre with high-speed courtroom battles and eccentric character designs.

His fingerprints are highly visible across the dialogue and pacing of this new project.

He is not writing alone.

Takumi Nakazawa, the mind behind the cult classic Ever 17 - The Out of Infinity, brings his expertise in branching narratives and science fiction concepts.

Takekuni Kitayama (Master Detective Archives: RAIN CODE) rounds out the writing trio.

The distinct, sharp-edged character artwork comes from illustrator simadoriru, while the soundtrack features the energetic, electronic beats of composer Masafumi Takada.

Anyone familiar with the audio landscape of The Hundred Line -Last Defense Academy will immediately recognize Takada’s audio signatures here.

Pre-Order Bonuses and Digital Alternatives

Physical releases often come with physical perks, and this game is no exception.

Early buyers who secured the standard Switch pre-order were promised a physical prequel novel titled Before the Murders case: Manji Fushicho.

Kodaka supervised the text for this book, which provides critical backstory on the cult’s hierarchy before the main game begins.

If you pre-ordered through a major retailer, this delay should not affect your bonus allocation.

The books ship inside or alongside the game cases.

If a late April delivery completely ruins your weekend plans, the digital route remains wide open.

Spike Chunsoft sells a Digital Deluxe version on both Steam and the Nintendo eShop.

This package includes a digital artbook and the game’s soundtrack.

The State of Niche Imports on New Hardware

Releasing a heavily localized, multi-genre adventure game early in a console’s life cycle is a calculated risk.

System launches usually prioritize big-budget action titles to show off graphical capabilities.

Shuten Order tests the waters for narrative-driven games on the Switch 2.

The visual novel audience is incredibly loyal to Nintendo’s portable ecosystem.

So yes, by ensuring a boxed copy exists for the new hardware, publisher DMM Games and Spike Chunsoft guarantee that hardcore collectors can populate their fresh Switch 2 shelves immediately.

Physical game preservation remains a hot topic in 2026.

As digital storefronts become more crowded, obtaining a cartridge ensures you own the software regardless of future server shutdowns or licensing expirations.

A one-week wait is a small price to pay for permanent ownership.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Is the digital version of Shuten Order also delayed?

No. The digital editions for PC and Nintendo Switch launched globally on September 5, 2025. This delay only impacts the newly printed retail boxes in North America.

Why is the European release date earlier than North America?

European distribution is handled by a different company, REEF Entertainment. Their freight shipments cleared logistics on time, allowing them to hit the original April 23 target.

Does the Switch 2 version cost more than the standard Switch version?

Yes. The physical Nintendo Switch 2 edition carries an MSRP of $59.99, while the standard Switch version costs $49.99 in North America.

Can I still get the prequel novel pre-order bonus?

Yes, pre-order allocations for the physical novel Before the Murders case: Manji Fushicho remain intact. Retailers will hold the bonuses and ship them alongside the game cases on the new April 30 release date.

What languages are supported on the cartridge?

The game features Japanese voice acting. Text options include English, Japanese, Traditional Chinese, and Simplified Chinese.
