Nintendo Launches Free 'Hello, Yoshi!' App: A Zero-Ad Game for Toddlers

The newest addition to the "My Mario" lineup gives parents a completely free, microtransaction-free way to introduce young children to the Mushroom Kingdom.

By Christopher Ivanov
Apr 10, 2026

Nintendo officially released the free Hello, Yoshi! app on April 9 across the Nintendo Switch, iOS, and Android platforms. Designed specifically for toddlers and young children, this interactive software lets users play directly with the iconic green dinosaur using simple touchscreen controls.

The software arrives as the latest digital offering under the “My Mario” banner, a dedicated product line built to introduce early-childhood demographics to the company’s most recognizable characters.

Parents looking for safe, ad-free entertainment can download the title today without worrying about hidden paywalls or complicated control schemes.

Interactive screen mechanics form the core experience. Children can poke, stretch, and tickle Yoshi’s face, triggering various animated reactions and sound effects.

This mirrors the exact gameplay loop established by its predecessor, Hello, Mario!, which hit United States storefronts earlier this winter.

Understanding the “My Mario” Strategy

Nintendo is actively targeting a younger generation.

The My Mario initiative represents a calculated business shift toward capturing brand loyalty during the toddler years.

Instead of relying solely on complex platforming games, the publisher is building a multimedia ecosystem.

This framework includes digital applications, physical merchandise, and short-form video content tailored for short attention spans.

The digital rollout actually began overseas.

Japanese markets received the initial Mario-themed app last August, followed by the Yoshi equivalent on November 18. American audiences gained access to the Mario version on February 19.

Now the Yoshi iteration brings the US market up to speed.

These applications serve as a gateway.

Because they are completely devoid of monetization traps, parents feel comfortable leaving their toddlers with a smartphone or a Switch console in handheld mode.

The tactile feedback helps develop basic motor skills while familiarizing kids with the colorful cast of the Mushroom Kingdom.

Stop-Motion Animation on YouTube

The brand expansion extends directly to video streaming.

The official Play Nintendo YouTube channel currently hosts a series of kid-friendly, one-minute animated shorts.

These bite-sized episodes utilize stop-motion animation, giving the characters a distinct, toy-like aesthetic that separates them from the glossy 3D models seen in mainline video games.

Episode Seven officially debuted on April 9, coinciding perfectly with the software launch. The short runtimes are deliberately crafted to hold a toddler’s focus without causing screen-time fatigue.

Expanding Physical Merchandise

Digital releases only represent half of the current corporate strategy.

Nintendo paired the February US app launch with a significant physical merchandise push at its flagship retail locations in New York and San Francisco.

The current My Mario product catalog features partnerships with several major manufacturers:

While initially restricted to the company’s two premier coastal stores, this exclusivity is ending.

Consumers across the country will find these products at select retail chains nationwide this spring.

This wider distribution signals strong internal confidence in the baby-focused product line.

Yoshi Takes Center Stage in 2026

The green dinosaur is having a banner year.

Beyond the smartphone space, the character is positioned as a primary focus across multiple high-profile entertainment sectors.

Gamers are currently preparing for the next major hardware generation.

Yoshi and the Mysterious Book is officially slated as a launch-window title for the Nintendo Switch 2.

The new console software will release on May 21.

While gameplay specifics remain tightly guarded, the timing aligns perfectly with the recent toddler-focused marketing blitz, ensuring the character remains highly visible right before the new console hits store shelves.

Box Office Dominance and the Galaxy Sequel

Hollywood is also banking heavily on the franchise.

The Super Mario Galaxy Movie opened in North American theaters on April 3, with a Japanese theatrical debut scheduled for April 24.

This blockbuster sequel features Donald Glover joining the established voice cast to portray Yoshi.

Casting a high-profile celebrity emphasizes the studio’s commitment to making the sidekick a central figure in the cinematic universe.

Expectations for the sequel are stratospheric, largely due to the historic performance of its predecessor.

The original Super Mario Bros. Movie hit theaters in April 2023 and completely rewrote the record books.

It generated $31,702,735 on its opening day alone in the United States.

Because it premiered on a Wednesday, the feature enjoyed a lucrative five-day opening frame, pulling in $204,630,730 domestically.

According to Deadline, the 2023 film secured an estimated $377,628,865 worldwide during that initial five-day run.

This shattered previous industry benchmarks, dethroning Frozen II to claim the highest-earning all-time worldwide opening for an animated feature.

The financial momentum never slowed down.

The adaptation finished 2023 as the second-highest-earning film globally and domestically.

Final tallies show a staggering $1,361,367,353 in total worldwide earnings, with $574,934,330 originating from American ticket sales.

It currently holds the undisputed title of the highest-earning video game adaptation in cinematic history.

Why Parents Should Download Hello, Yoshi!

Navigating the modern app store is incredibly frustrating for parents.

The mobile marketplace is flooded with software that disguises aggressive microtransactions behind bright colors and cartoon animals.

Nintendo entirely sidesteps this issue.

The Hello, Yoshi! download requires zero financial investment after the initial installation.

There are no prompts to buy extra lives, unlock new outfits, or watch unskippable advertisements.

The user interface is intentionally minimalist.

Large buttons, bold colors, and immediate auditory feedback reward curious fingers.

This clean design philosophy ensures that a two-year-old can navigate the software independently, providing caretakers with a rare moment of peace.

Where to Download the Game

Acquiring the software takes only a few minutes.

Consumers can find the title across three major digital storefronts:

It cannot be played while the console is docked and connected to a television monitor.

The Future of Early Childhood Gaming

The video game industry historically struggled to design compelling experiences for children under five.

Traditional controllers demand a level of dexterity that toddlers simply do not possess.

Smartphones fundamentally changed that dynamic.

The intuitive nature of a touch-sensitive glass panel eliminated the hardware barrier.

Nintendo is capitalizing on this reality by building a safe, recognizable bridge between early development toys and formal video games.

Expect the Japanese publisher to aggressively expand this sector.

If the physical merchandise sells well at mainstream retail stores this spring, more legacy characters will likely receive their own dedicated digital sandboxes.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Is the Hello, Yoshi! app completely free?

Yes. The software is free to download and contains absolutely no hidden microtransactions, subscriptions, or in-app purchases. It is designed as a safe environment for young children.

What devices can run the new Yoshi game?

You can install the software on iOS devices via the App Store, Android devices via Google Play, and on the Nintendo Switch console. Note that the Switch version requires handheld mode due to touchscreen functionality.

What is the My Mario brand?

It is a specialized product line created by Nintendo targeting infants and toddlers. The umbrella covers digital apps, one-minute stop-motion YouTube cartoons, picture books, clothing, and physical toys.

Who plays Yoshi in the new movie?

Actor and musician Donald Glover provides the voice for the character in The Super Mario Galaxy Movie, which premiered in North American theaters on April 3.

When does the next major Yoshi console game release?

Yoshi and the Mysterious Book is scheduled to launch on May 21. It will be available on the Nintendo Switch 2 hardware system.
