One Piece Episode 1156 Preview: The Straw Hats Finally Arrive in Elbaph (Release Date & Trailer Details)

The unprecedented 3-month hiatus is finally over. Here is exactly what to expect as the Straw Hats land on Elbaph, plus details on Prince Loki’s highly anticipated debut.

By Christopher Ivanov
Mar 31, 2026

The wait is finally over. One Piece Episode 1156 officially marks the end of the anime’s unprecedented three-month hiatus, bringing the Straw Hat Pirates to the highly anticipated land of giants: Elbaph.

Titled “The Long-sought Elbaph! The Big Reunion Banquet,” the episode airs next week in Japan, on April 5, 2026, and kicks off the next major saga with fresh theme songs and the highly anticipated debut of Prince Loki.

If you’ve been tracking the anime’s production, you know that Toei Animation taking a seasonal break is a massive deal.

It signals a major shift in how the studio handles pacing and animation quality.

What to Expect in One Piece Episode 1156

The newly released preview trailer for Episode 1156 (watch below) gives us our first animated look at the towering landscapes of Elbaph.

After the chaotic events of Egghead Island, the crew is moving into a territory that has been teased for over two decades.

Here is what the preview and recent studio announcements confirm for the upcoming premiere:

  • A Shift in Visuals: The preview showcases a distinct color palette tailored to Elbaph’s Nordic-inspired, mythological aesthetic.
  • The Big Reunion: The episode title, “The Big Reunion Banquet,” points toward the Straw Hats reconnecting with familiar giant faces.
  • Pacing Corrections: The three-month break allowed the manga to build a comfortable lead. This means Episode 1156 should feature tighter pacing, avoiding the dragged-out scenes that sometimes plague weekly anime.

For general viewers, this episode acts as a perfect jumping-on point.

It transitions the crew from the sci-fi heavy themes of Egghead directly into a high-fantasy, adventure-focused setting.

Why Did the Anime Take a 3-Month Break?

Historically the One Piece anime runs continuously, 52 weeks a year. Taking a full broadcast season off is incredibly rare.

From an industry perspective, this was a strategic move by the production committee. By pausing the broadcast, Toei Animation accomplished two things.

  1. First, they gave Eiichiro Oda’s manga time to get further ahead, preventing the anime from catching up to the source material.
  2. Second, it provided the animators a much-needed buffer to maintain the movie-quality visuals we saw during the climax of Wano and Egghead.

When Episode 1156 drops, expect top-tier animation and storyboarding. The studio has had three months to polish this debut.

Enter Prince Loki (or Why Yuichi Nakamura’s Casting is a Big Deal)

As the Elbaph arc approaches, hype has been building around new character designs and their respective voice actors.

The standout announcement is the casting of Yuichi Nakamura as the voice of Prince Loki.

If you are an anime fan, you already know Nakamura’s voice.

He is an industry heavyweight known for voicing some of the most popular characters in modern anime.

Notable roles by Yuichi Nakamura include:

  • Satoru Gojo (Jujutsu Kaisen)
  • Hawks (My Hero Academia)
  • Tetsurou Kuroo (Haikyuu!!)
  • Mumen Rider (One Punch Man)

Casting an A-lister like Nakamura guarantees that Prince Loki is not just a background royal.

Loki is being positioned as a central, highly charismatic figure who will play a massive role in the final saga of the series.

Nakamura excels at playing characters who are charming, dangerous, and deeply complex, traits that perfectly align with Loki’s reputation in the manga.

Fresh Music (New Opening and Ending Themes)

A new island means a new soundtrack. To celebrate the end of the hiatus and the beginning of the Elbaph Saga, One Piece is refreshing its musical lineup.

  • New Opening Theme: “Luminous” performed by AiNA THE END.
  • New Ending Theme: “That Future” performed by Jisoku 36km.

AiNA THE END’s distinct, raspy vocal style (previously heard in Mobile Suit Gundam: The Witch from Mercury and Apocrypha) is a fantastic fit for the grand, slightly darker, and mythical tone that Elbaph promises.

Moving away from the upbeat, pop-centric themes of Egghead, “Luminous” will likely set a more epic, sprawling mood for the giant’s homeland.

A 25-Year Journey

To understand the SEO search volume and fan hype surrounding Episode 1156, you have to understand the history of Elbaph in the One Piece lore.

This is more than just another island on the Grand Line, oh no sir, this is a destination that was first mentioned all the way back in the year 2000 during the Little Garden Arc.

Usopp’s Ultimate Dream

For Usopp, Elbaph represents his ultimate character goal.

Ever since he met the giant captains Dorry and Brogy, Usopp has idolized the warriors of Elbaph.

His dream to become a “brave warrior of the sea” is intrinsically tied to visiting this island.

Fans have waited a quarter of a century to see Usopp step foot on this soil, making this arc a massive emotional payoff.

The Shanks Connection

Elbaph is closely associated with Red-Haired Shanks, one of the Four Emperors of the Sea.

Recent manga chapters have shown that Shanks has strong ties to the giants and uses their territory as a safe haven.

With the Straw Hats arriving on Elbaph, the inevitable reunion between Monkey D. Luffy and his childhood mentor feels closer than ever.

The Lore of the Void Century

Beyond personal character arcs, Elbaph is a treasure trove of world history.

Giants live for hundreds of years, meaning their elders have connections to the mysterious Void Century.

From the survival of Jaguar D. Saul to the preservation of the Ohara library, Elbaph holds the answers to some of the biggest mysteries Eiichiro Oda has ever written.

Beyond Episode 1156 (The Future of the One Piece Franchise)

The One Piece franchise is currently experiencing a global renaissance.

While the mainline anime’s return with Episode 1156 is the main event, the production committees at Shueisha and Toei are aggressively expanding the IP across multiple mediums.

Here is a breakdown of the major One Piece projects currently in the pipeline:

1. Netflix’s Live-Action Series (Season 3 Confirmed)

Following the massive, record-breaking success of Season 1 and the highly anticipated production of Season 2 (covering Alabasta), Netflix has already greenlit Season 3.

The live-action adaptation has successfully bridged the gap between hardcore anime fans and general American audiences, proving that Oda’s wacky world can translate to live-action if handled with respect.

2. The WIT Studio Remake (“The One Piece”)

Perhaps the most ambitious project on the horizon is the upcoming anime remake titled simply, The One Piece.

Produced by WIT Studio (the powerhouse behind the early seasons of Attack on Titan and Spy x Family), this project aims to re-animate the East Blue saga from scratch.

Why a remake? The original 1999 anime, while beloved, suffers from outdated 4:3 aspect ratios, old animation techniques, and filler pacing. WIT Studio’s version will offer a modern, fast-paced alternative for new viewers who find the 1,100+ episode count too intimidating.

3. One Piece: Heroines Anime Adaptation

The franchise is also expanding its spin-off material.

The One Piece: Heroines novel, which focuses on the daily lives and unseen adventures of the series’ prominent female characters (like Nami, Robin, Vivi, and Boa Hancock), is officially receiving an anime adaptation.

This project will give fans a closer, slice-of-life look at these characters outside of the usual high-stakes battles.

Conclusion

Calling Episode 1156 a ‘return from hiatus’ feels like a massive understatement.

We’ve been waiting since the early 2000s to see the Straw Hats actually pull up to Elbaph, and the timing couldn’t be better.

With Toei finally giving the animators some breathing room to deliver movie-tier visuals and Nakamura stepping into the booth as Prince Loki, the series is moving into its endgame with some serious momentum.

If you’ve been looking for an excuse to get hyped again, this is it.

So make sure to tune in next week (April 5, 2026) to catch the debut of “The Long-sought Elbaph! The Big Reunion Banquet” on Crunchyroll.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

When does One Piece Episode 1156 release?

One Piece Episode 1156 is scheduled to air next week in Japan, on April 5, 2026, marking the end of the anime’s three-month broadcast hiatus. It will be available to stream globally on Crunchyroll shortly after its Japanese broadcast.

Why was the One Piece anime on a 3-month break?

Toei Animation placed the anime on a three-month hiatus to improve pacing and allow Eiichiro Oda’s manga to get further ahead. This ensures the anime can adapt the Elbaph arc with high-quality animation without resorting to filler episodes.

Who is the voice actor for Prince Loki in One Piece?

Prince Loki is voiced by Yuichi Nakamura, a highly popular voice actor known for his roles as Satoru Gojo in Jujutsu Kaisen and Hawks in My Hero Academia.

Is Elbaph the final arc in One Piece?

While Elbaph is part of the “Final Saga” of the One Piece story, it is not confirmed to be the final arc. It is a major stepping stone that will likely lead directly into the series’ ultimate climax and the discovery of the One Piece treasure.

What are the new theme songs for the Elbaph Arc?

The new opening theme is “Luminous” by AiNA THE END, and the new ending theme is “That Future” by Jisoku 36km. These songs will debut alongside Episode 1156.
