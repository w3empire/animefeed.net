The Oblivious Saint Can’t Contain Her Power Anime Reveals Edward Visual and July 2026 Premiere

Voice actor Makoto Furukawa joins the summer fantasy romance as the feared but fiercely protective Bloodthirsty Prince.

By Justin Ali
Apr 17, 2026

The Oblivious Saint Can’t Contain Her Power anime adaptation just released a brand-new character concept visual featuring the “Bloodthirsty Prince” Edward. Slated for a July 2026 television release, this upcoming fantasy romance offers viewers a closer look at its intense male lead. The Japanese broadcast will air across Tokyo MX, BS11, and AT-X. Plus the production committee confirmed that veteran voice actor Makoto Furukawa will bring Edward to life.

The latest promotional image highlights Edward’s resolute, determined expression. An official caption accompanies the artwork: “It’s all right now. I will protect Carolina no matter what.”

This specific dialogue establishes the core emotional drive of his character. He transitions from a feared military figure into a dedicated protector. Fans of the original light novels know this evolving dynamic forms the emotional backbone of the entire narrative.

Who is Prince Edward in The Oblivious Saint?

Within the story, Edward holds a terrifying reputation on the battlefield.

The citizens of the Empire of Malcosias know him strictly as a ruthless commander.

Political rumors paint him as an unfeeling warrior who crushes his enemies without hesitation.

But his relationship with Carolina slowly reveals a completely different side to his personality.

The newly released concept visual focuses heavily on this hidden tenderness.

The harsh exterior hides a man desperate to keep his new bride safe.

Voice actor Makoto Furukawa brings significant gravity to this complex role.

American anime fans likely recognize him from iconic vocal performances, such as Saitama in One Punch Man.

He excels at playing characters who possess overwhelming physical strength but hide a quiet vulnerability.

The official anime website recently published Furukawa’s cast commentary.

He emphasized the layered approach he takes to the prince’s dialogue during recording sessions.

His impressive vocal range perfectly suits a hardened warrior discovering a soft heart.

His performance will likely elevate Edward from a standard romance interest into a compelling, multi-dimensional co-lead.

Note on the Voice Cast: If you watched Kaguya-sama: Love is War (he played Miyuki Shirogane), you already know Furukawa is the undisputed king of voicing guys who project a tough, intimidating exterior but are actually complete softies on the inside. Nabbing him for Edward is a brilliant move by the studio. It practically guarantees they are focusing heavily on the sweet, romantic payoff rather than just the grim political drama.

Plot Breakdown (The Hidden Power of Lady Carolina)

J-Novel Club currently handles the English publication for the franchise’s light novel and manga.

Their official English synopsis outlines a classic, highly satisfying underdog narrative.

Lady Carolina lives entirely under the shadow of her famous, aristocratic family.

Her father serves as the distinguished prime minister, while her older sister stands as a prodigy mage next in line for the holy title of Saint.

Feeling entirely overlooked, Carolina accepts a quiet, unremarkable life.

A sudden royal decree completely disrupts her peaceful existence.

She faces a forced political marriage to Edward and must relocate immediately.

Instead of surrendering to despair, she steps boldly into a highly dangerous political landscape.

The plot effectively blends court intrigue with steady romantic development.

As Carolina navigates life in the Malcosias Empire, two crucial things occur.

She discovers hints of true love with her supposedly terrifying husband.

More importantly, her own latent magical abilities begin to awaken.

She slowly realizes exactly what it means to be truly exceptional.

Analyzing the Malcosias Empire Setting

The Empire of Malcosias serves as a harsh, unforgiving backdrop for this romance.

Unlike Carolina’s refined homeland, this neighboring nation strictly values military might and tactical dominance.

Sending a delicate duke’s daughter across the border feels like a deliberate political exile.

This geographical shift physically isolates our protagonist from her safety net.

Carolina must rely entirely on her own wits to survive.

The mortal danger she faces accelerates her bond with Edward.

The characters are forced to trust one another to navigate enemy schemes and assassination attempts.

This dual-focus on romantic tension and physical survival elevates the material beyond a simple slice-of-life fairy tale.

The “hidden saint” narrative currently dominates the light novel publication cycle.

Audiences love watching an unappreciated character slowly reveal world-altering magical abilities.

Carolina’s journey captures this satisfying progression perfectly.

Because her older sister holds the official spotlight back home, Carolina’s eventual rise to power feels significantly more impactful.

Animation Studio and Key Production Staff

A highly capable creative team is bringing this story to television screens.

Co-studios Magic Bus and Picante Circus are jointly handling the animation production.

They aim to translate Yoshiro Ambe’s detailed original book illustrations into a fluid, broadcast-ready format.

Their collaborative effort ensures the character designs remain highly faithful to the source material.

He previously directed the fantasy series The Strongest Tank’s Labyrinth Raids.

His direct experience managing fantasy settings with heavy action elements provides a solid foundation for this new show.

He understands how to frame both magical combat and quiet, intimate conversations.

She previously worked on the hit slice-of-life show Farming Life in Another World.

Her involvement guarantees a well-structured script that balances relaxing character moments with urgent plot progression.

Why This Fantasy Romance Stands Out

The Japanese anime market sees dozens of new fantasy titles premiere every single season.

Yet, The Oblivious Saint Can’t Contain Her Power offers a specific, refined appeal for romance fans.

It actively avoids the common trap of utilizing a completely helpless protagonist.

Carolina works incredibly hard to prove her worth despite the dangerous circumstances surrounding her.

The emotional dynamic between the two leads feels earned rather than forced.

Edward’s intimidating persona creates high initial tension during their first meeting.

Watching that icy tension melt into genuine affection provides a highly satisfying character arc.

Original author Almond paced the light novel narrative perfectly to maximize this specific emotional payoff.

A Guide to the Original Light Novels and Manga

Impatient viewers do not have to wait for the summer television season.

The original light novels are already available to read in English right now.

J-Novel Club offers high-quality digital translations for overseas readers.

Reading the books ahead of time provides excellent context for the upcoming animated adaptation.

The official manga adaptation also provides a fantastic entry point for newcomers.

It visualizes the key story beats with beautiful, expressive artwork.

Many fans prefer reading the comic format to clearly see the character expressions firsthand.

Both mediums offer a great way to experience Carolina’s inspiring journey today.

Tip for Starting the Series: If you want to jump into the story before the July premiere, start with the manga. It paces the early world-building a bit faster, making it a super easy weekend binge. But if you are a fan of deep, slow-burn romance, switch over to the light novels by Volume 2. The novels give you way more of Carolina’s internal thoughts and the subtle political maneuvering that the comic just doesn’t have room to fit on the page.

Supporting the official English releases directly helps guarantee future anime seasons.

Strong western sales numbers frequently influence the decisions of production committees in Japan.

If you enjoy political fantasy romance, grabbing the first volume is highly recommended.

Conclusion

As someone who spends all day tracking anime news and reading endless light novel summaries, I see a lot of cookie-cutter fantasy shows cross my desk.

It takes quite a bit to catch my eye these days, but the upcoming July release of The Oblivious Saint Can’t Contain Her Power genuinely feels like a breath of fresh air.

I’ve read my fair share of romance manga over the years, and a lot of them fall flat when it comes to believable relationships.

The dynamic between Carolina and Edward trades the usual instant-love tropes for a much more rewarding, slow-burn underdog story.

Watching two people actually work as a team to survive political backstabbing is just good storytelling.

If you are tired of helpless protagonists and want a female lead who actively steps up to the plate, this show is going to hit the spot.

The voice cast is absolutely stacked, the animation studios know how to handle high-stakes action, and the source material already has a fiercely loyal fanbase for a reason.

I highly recommend grabbing the manga for a quick weekend read before the summer season kicks off.

It gives you a great feel for the harsh Malcosias Empire without spoiling the whole ride.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

When does The Oblivious Saint Can’t Contain Her Power anime premiere?

The series officially debuts in July 2026. It will air on Japanese television networks including Tokyo MX, BS11, and AT-X. Streaming platform details for North America are expected closer to the release date.

Who is the voice actor for Prince Edward?

Veteran Japanese voice actor Makoto Furukawa plays the role of the “Bloodthirsty Prince” Edward. You can find his official cast commentary regarding the role on the anime’s official Japanese website.

What is the plot of The Oblivious Saint Can’t Contain Her Power?

The story follows Lady Carolina, the overlooked daughter of a prime minister. She is forced into a political marriage with Prince Edward of the rival Malcosias Empire. Together, they navigate political danger while her own hidden magical powers slowly awaken.

Where can I read the original story in English?

J-Novel Club publishes the official English translations for both the light novel series and the manga adaptation. Digital copies are currently available for purchase across all major ebook storefronts.

What studio is animating the new series?

The television adaptation is a joint production between animation studios Magic Bus and Picante Circus. The overall project is directed by Mitsutaka Noshitani.
